# External review guide

The starter kit can prove file contracts, query outputs, provenance fields, and
negative fixtures. It cannot prove that a scientific term mapping, historical
summary, consultancy workflow, or regulated quality dependency is correct for a
real organization. The register in `domain-review-register.yaml` keeps those
claims open until a named reviewer signs them.

For each entry, a reviewer should record:

1. reviewer name, role, organization, and date;
2. the exact files and fixture version reviewed;
3. accepted findings, rejected findings, and unresolved questions;
4. any terminology, evidence, licensing, or regulatory limitation;
5. a decision of `approved`, `approved-with-changes`, or `rejected`.

An approval applies to the reviewed synthetic lesson and its stated scope. It
does not imply McKinsey, PwC, a client, a regulator, a clinical study, or a
published business result adopted the starter kit.
