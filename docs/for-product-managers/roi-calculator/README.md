# ROI Calculator

This module helps product managers build a defensible business case for ontology-backed AI features.

## What It Includes

- [ROI template](file:///Users/samiol/Desktop/Ontology/ontology-product-starter/docs/for-product-managers/roi-calculator/roi-template.md): formulas for costs, savings, ROI, NPV, and payback period.
- [Investment one-pager](file:///Users/samiol/Desktop/Ontology/ontology-product-starter/docs/for-product-managers/investment-one-pager.md): a concise executive summary template.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Estimate build and maintenance costs with engineering and domain leads.
2. Quantify current pain from hallucinations, manual reconciliation, and delayed feature delivery.
3. Fill in the ROI template with a 12- or 36-month horizon.
4. Convert the result into the investment one-pager for leadership review.

## Important Caveat

The sample figures in this folder are illustrative. Replace them with values from your own product, team costs, and risk profile before sharing externally.
